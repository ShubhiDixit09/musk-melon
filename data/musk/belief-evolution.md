# Elon Musk — Belief Evolution Timeline

This document tracks how Elon Musk's publicly stated positions evolved over time.
Each entry is sourced from tweets, interviews, or public statements.

## On Cryptocurrency / Bitcoin

### 2018-2019: Cautious Interest
- "I literally own zero cryptocurrency, apart from .25 BTC a friend sent me many years ago" (Feb 2018)
- Generally neutral, focused on Tesla/SpaceX

### 2021 (Jan-May): Strong Pro-Bitcoin
- Tesla bought $1.5B in Bitcoin (Feb 2021)
- "You can now buy a Tesla with Bitcoin" (Mar 2021)
- Added #Bitcoin to Twitter bio
- "Bitcoin is a good thing" — multiple supportive tweets

### 2021 (May-Jun): Anti-Bitcoin Reversal
- "Tesla has suspended vehicle purchases using Bitcoin" due to energy concerns (May 2021)
- Promoted Dogecoin instead — "Dogecoin is the people's crypto"
- Bitcoin crashed after his tweets

### 2022-2025: Selective Crypto Support
- Continued Dogecoin support
- Tesla holds Bitcoin on balance sheet but doesn't accept for purchases
- Focused more on DOGE memes

**Key Contradiction**: Pro-Bitcoin → Anti-Bitcoin (energy concerns) within 3 months in 2021.

## On Artificial Intelligence

### 2014-2018: AI Doomer
- "With artificial intelligence, we are summoning the demon" (Oct 2014)
- Co-founded OpenAI to ensure safe AI development (2015)
- "AI is far more dangerous than nukes" (Mar 2018)
- Left OpenAI board (2018)

### 2019-2022: Nuanced Concern
- "AI doesn't need to hate us to destroy us" (2019)
- Continued warnings but acknowledged AI's benefits
- Tesla's FSD relied on AI — implicit endorsement of practical AI

### 2023-2025: AI Builder & Competitor
- Founded xAI (Jul 2023)
- Built Grok chatbot
- Sued OpenAI for abandoning its nonprofit mission
- Now building AI actively while still warning about AGI risks

**Key Contradiction**: Warned AI could destroy humanity → Founded an AI company. Justification: "If you can't beat them, join them" + belief his AI would be less biased.

## On Free Speech

### Pre-2022: General Advocate
- "I'm a free speech absolutist" (various tweets)
- Criticized mainstream media and censorship

### 2022: Bought Twitter
- Acquired Twitter for $44B specifically for free speech
- "The bird is freed" (Oct 2022)
- Unbanned many previously suspended accounts
- Rebranded to X

### 2023-2025: Complex Implementation
- Faced backlash from advertisers
- Balanced free speech with content moderation challenges
- Suspended journalists' accounts briefly (contradiction with absolutist stance)
- Complied with some government takedown requests

**Key Evolution**: Abstract principle → Practical implementation revealed tradeoffs.

## On Mars Colonization

### 2010-2015: Ambitious Timeline
- "Humans on Mars by 2024" (various early statements)
- SpaceX's raison d'être: making humanity multi-planetary

### 2016-2020: Revised but Committed
- Timelines pushed back repeatedly
- Starship development began
- "2026 for uncrewed missions, 2028 for crewed" (approx)

### 2021-2025: Long-Term Vision Maintained
- Starship orbital flights achieved
- "City on Mars by 2050" as the stable long-term goal
- Focus shifted to Starship as the key enabler

**Key Pattern**: Specific dates always optimistic, but direction never wavered.

## On Government & Politics

### 2010-2019: Moderate/Slightly Left-Leaning
- Supported Obama-era policies
- "I'm somewhat left of center"
- Focused on climate change advocacy

### 2020-2021: COVID Friction
- "FREE AMERICA NOW" (Apr 2020) — anti-lockdown
- Moved Tesla HQ from California to Texas
- Began criticizing Democrat policies

### 2022-2024: Sharp Right Turn
- "I used to be left of center, now the left has gone so far left it made me look right"
- Publicly endorsed Trump (2024)
- Became vocal on immigration, woke culture criticism

### 2025: Government Involvement
- Brief role in government efficiency (DOGE)
- Then stepped back from direct government role

**Key Shift**: Climate/tech moderate → Anti-establishment conservative. Driven by COVID lockdowns, California regulation frustration, and culture war engagement.

## On Tesla's Mission

### 2010-2015: Sustainable Energy Mission
- "Tesla's mission is to accelerate the world's transition to sustainable energy"
- Focus on electric cars, solar, batteries

### 2016-2020: Autonomous Driving Push
- "Full Self-Driving next year" (said almost every year)
- FSD promises repeatedly delayed
- Production hell with Model 3

### 2021-2025: AI & Robotics Expansion
- Tesla pivoted to "AI robotics company"
- Optimus humanoid robot
- Robotaxi concept
- Still making cars but narrative shifted

**Key Pattern**: Core mission stable, but emphasis shifted from cars → AI/robotics over time.
